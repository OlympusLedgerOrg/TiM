# How to Complete a Work Order Step

**What it does:** Mark a step as done, leave optional notes, and notify your supervisor in real time.

## Find the Step
1. Open **Work Orders**.
2. Select the correct work order.
3. Scroll to the step you’re finishing.

## Complete the Step
1. Click **Complete Step**.
2. Add notes (optional but recommended).
3. Confirm.

**Heads-up:** Completed steps can’t be undone. Your supervisor will be notified automatically.
